## Abstract

Intelligent Energy Consumption Analytics for Smart Buildings is a data-driven system designed to monitor, understand, and improve the way energy is consumed in modern buildings. The system collects energy usage information from smart meters and other building data sources and processes it to identify meaningful patterns. Historical consumption, time-based information, occupancy, environmental conditions, and equipment usage can be analyzed to predict future energy requirements. The proposed solution also detects unusual consumption patterns that may indicate equipment faults, unexpected usage, or energy wastage. By combining prediction, anomaly detection, wastage analysis, and recommendation techniques, the system can provide useful suggestions for reducing unnecessary consumption. The overall objective is to help building managers make informed decisions, reduce energy costs, improve operational efficiency, and support sustainable energy management.

## Project Description

Smart buildings use connected devices, sensors, smart meters, and automated systems to improve building operations. The proposed system uses data processing and machine learning techniques to understand energy consumption behavior and forecast future demand. It analyzes daily, weekly, and seasonal patterns and considers factors such as occupancy, temperature, time, and equipment operation.

The system focuses on:

* Energy consumption prediction
* Anomaly detection
* Energy wastage analysis
* Energy-saving recommendations
